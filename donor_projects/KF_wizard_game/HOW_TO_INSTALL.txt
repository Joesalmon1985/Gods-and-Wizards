Godot 4.4.1 patch — PlayerCharacter + DebugSpectator + non-blocking combat UI.

1) Copy everything under res:// from this zip into your project, keeping folders:
   autoload/, ui/, actors/, scenes/, scripts/, patches/

2) Project > Project Settings > Autoload:
   Add:  res://autoload/UIManager.gd  as  UIManager  (Enabled)

3) Input Map (Project Settings > Input Map). Add if missing:
   move_forward (W), move_backward (S), move_left (A), move_right (D),
   sprint (Shift), ui_cancel (Esc),
   move_up (E/Space), move_down (Q/C), crouch (Ctrl), toggle_hud (H)

4) Scenes:
   - Main game: open res://scenes/MainGame.tscn (spawns PlayerCharacter).
   - Debug sandbox: open res://scenes/DebugSandbox.tscn (spawns free-fly spectator).

5) Player spawn:
   Place a Node3D/Marker3D named "PlayerSpawn" in your level (res://scenes/GameLevel.tscn)
   or add a node to group "player_spawn". The spawner will place the player there.

6) Combat UI:
   When an Encounter turn belongs to a node in group "player", CardCombatEncounter will
   call UIManager.begin_card_choice() to show the hand (no 'await' inside step()).

7) Patching CardCombatEncounter:
   Try the unified diff in patches/CardCombatEncounter_player_input.diff against your file.
   If your path differs, copy encounters/CardCombatEncounter_with_player_input.gd and manually
   merge the 'non-blocking player choice' parts into your script.

Notes:
  - All GDScript avoids the '? :' operator and uses 4.4.1-friendly constructs.
  - If your Character3D isn't CharacterBody3D, move your FPS movement from FpsPlayer.gd into
    PlayerCharacter.gd or compose it as a separate controller script.
